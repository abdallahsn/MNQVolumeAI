# Algorithm Selection Policy

Verification date: 2026-07-11

Before choosing an algorithm, adopting a paper, or adding a research method, invoke `$quant-research-scout`.

Algorithm adoption requires:

- a foundational simple baseline;
- a recent and foundational literature review from primary sources;
- causal compatibility with known-at-time market data;
- reproducible implementation details;
- chronological out-of-sample comparison;
- cost-aware evaluation including fees, spread, slippage, and latency;
- rejection of novelty-only decisions.

Do not transfer reported results from ES, NQ, equities, crypto, FX, or any other market to MNQ without independent validation. Reported win rates, profit factors, Sharpe values, and drawdowns remain unverified until reproduced here.

“Latest” is not an acceptance criterion.

“Stable, compatible, causal, reproducible, and empirically superior” is the acceptance criterion.
